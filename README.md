### 🌟 Features  
- **Discover Any Car**:  
  🔍 Search by make, model, year, or fuel type. From vintage beauties to cutting-edge EVs, CarInside has it all!  
- **AI-Powered Assistance**:  
  🤖 Integrated with the ChatGPT API, our AI is always ready to assist—whether you’re browsing or searching for specifics.  
- **Beginner & Expert Modes**:  
  🛠️ Tailor your experience. Choose Beginner mode for simplicity or dive deep with Expert mode for all the intricate details.  
- **Immersive Experience**:  
  🎵 Explore high-resolution car images, detailed specifications, and listen to engine sounds for that true car enthusiast vibe.  
- **Adaptive Interface**:  
  📱 A seamless experience across iPhone, iPad, and Mac with a responsive and adaptive interface.  
- **Beautiful Animations**:  
  🎨 Every interaction feels smooth and delightful with handcrafted animations.  
- **Localization Support**:  
  🌍 CarInside is available in multiple languages.  
- **Built-in Browser**:  
  🌐 Explore car-related web content directly within the app.
